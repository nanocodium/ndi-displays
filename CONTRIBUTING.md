# Contributing

The full guide is on the wiki:

**[For developers](https://wiki.nailec.fr/devs/)** — [how to contribute](https://wiki.nailec.fr/devs/contributing) · [interop & API](https://wiki.nailec.fr/devs/interop)

Short version:

- One job per PR.
- Update **both** `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/reference/changelog.md` under `## [Unreleased]` ([Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)).
- Ship `ndidisplays-*-all.jar` only.
- Do not vendor Theatrical, Extra Lights, SEF, or a second NDI stack.
